Delete your Lunara account

You can delete your Lunara account and its data at any time, in the app or by email. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lunara and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap the settings icon in the top corner.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap Delete to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email lunaradailyapp@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Lunara account. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Lunara (for Google sign in, the address of that Google account), so we can confirm the account is yours. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to let you know it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign in account and the email address attached to it.
  • Your profile: your name, birth date, birth time and birth place.
  • Your journal entries and the daily prompts written for you.
  • Your readings, including your natal chart, Life Path, Becoming, daily readings and compatibility readings.
  • Your conversations with Luna.
  • Your friend code, your connections with friends, and any pending friend requests. You are removed from your friends' lists too.
  • Your app preferences and usage records.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records. If you subscribed to Lunara Premium, records of your purchases are kept by Google Play or the App Store, and by our payment processor, because they are needed for billing, refunds and tax. These are kept under their own retention policies.
  • Server logs. Routine system logs may include your account identifier and, occasionally, a short extract of a reading that failed to generate. They never include your journal entries, and they are deleted automatically within 30 days.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription

If you have Lunara Premium, cancel it first so you are not charged again. On Android, open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Questions about your data? Email lunaradailyapp@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
