Lunara Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 27, 2026

Lunara ("we", "us", "the app") is an astrology and tarot app for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what we collect, why, how it is stored, and who we share it with. We keep data collection to what the app needs to work.

Who we are
Lunara is made by the Lunara team. For privacy questions, contact us at lunaradailyapp@gmail.com.

The data we collect
Account. You sign in with your email and a password, with Google, or on iPhone with Apple. We receive your email address and, from Google or Apple, your name if you choose to share it. Firebase Authentication assigns your account a random user ID, which is how your data is stored and retrieved, so your data follows you to a new phone when you sign in.

Birth and profile details. To generate your readings, you give us a display name, your birth date, your birth place, and optionally your birth time. We use these to compute your sun sign, life path number, natal chart, and personalized readings. When you search for your birth city, the text you type is sent to Google Places to find it and its coordinates. You can also choose how Luna speaks to you (feminine, masculine, or neutral), which sets the Spanish wording of your readings. This information is stored in your profile.

People you check compatibility with. If you check your compatibility with someone, the name and birthday you enter for them are used to write that reading and are stored with it.

Readings and AI conversations. Daily horoscopes, tarot readings, crystal and color suggestions, affirmations, compatibility readings, your natal chart, your Life Path, your "Becoming" vision, your Orbit, and your conversations with Luna (our AI guide) are generated for you and stored so they load quickly and are not regenerated unnecessarily. Your Ask Luna messages and the AI replies are stored so your conversation has history.

Journal. Your journal entries and the mood you pick for each are stored in your account. They are never shown to friends. For Premium subscribers, short excerpts of recent entries are sent to Anthropic so Luna can refer to them.

Reports. If you report a reading or a reply from Luna, we store the reported text, the reason you chose, and any note you add, so we can review it.

Friends. If you use friends features, we store your friend code, the friend requests you send and receive, and your accepted friendships. Accepted friends can see your name, sun sign, friend code, and your reading for the day. They cannot see your birth details, your journal, or your conversations with Luna, and they cannot change your account.

Subscription status. If you subscribe to Lunara Premium, the App Store or Google Play processes the payment, and our subscription provider RevenueCat tells us whether your subscription is active. We never receive or store your payment card details.

Usage limits. We count how many readings and Luna questions you use each day or week, to apply the free limits and prevent abuse.

What we do not collect. Lunara does not read your device's location, does not use an advertising ID, and contains no advertising, analytics, or crash reporting tools.

How your data is used
To generate and personalize your astrology and tarot content.
To power Ask Luna conversations with memory of the discussion.
To run friends features (codes, requests, friendships, shared daily content).
To unlock Premium features for active subscribers.
To send the daily reminder notification, if you turn it on. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third parties we share data with
Lunara relies on a few service providers. Data is shared only as needed for the app to work:

Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions): signs you in, stores your profile, readings, conversations, journal, and friends, and runs our server functions. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
Anthropic (Claude AI): writes your readings and Luna's replies. The details a reading needs (such as your sun sign, birth details, the names and birthdays you enter for compatibility, your Ask Luna messages, and for Premium subscribers excerpts of your journal) are sent to Anthropic's API through our server. Our Anthropic key is held only on our server and never in the app. See https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
Google Places: finds your birth city as you type it. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.
RevenueCat (subscription management): keeps track of whether your subscription is active. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
Apple App Store and Google Play: process payments for Lunara Premium. See https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data retention and deletion
Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings, then Delete account, or by following the steps at https://lunara-application.github.io/lunara-legal/delete-account.html. Deleting your account permanently removes your sign-in, your profile, your readings, your Luna conversations, your journal, your friend code, friendships and friend requests, and your subscription record at RevenueCat. Reports you sent about AI content may be kept for review. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in the App Store or Google Play. Removing the app from your phone does not by itself delete your account.

Children
Lunara is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security
Access to your data is controlled by security rules tied to your signed-in account, all traffic is encrypted in transit, and sensitive operations run on our server. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on this page.
