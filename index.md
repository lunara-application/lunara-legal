Lunara Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 26, 2026

Lunara ("we", "us", "the app") is an astrology and tarot app. This policy explains what we collect, why, how it is stored, and who we share it with. We keep data collection to what the app needs to work.

Who we are
Lunara is operated by the Lunara team. For privacy questions, contact us at lunaradailyapp@gmail.com.

The data we collect
Account identity. Lunara signs you in anonymously. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to create an account. Firebase Authentication assigns your device a random anonymous user ID. This ID is how your data is stored and retrieved. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, a new anonymous ID is created and previous data may not carry over.

Birth and profile details. To generate your readings, you provide a display name, birth date, and optionally a birth time and birth location. We use these to compute your sun sign, life path number, natal chart, and personalized readings. This information is stored in your profile.

Readings and AI conversations. Daily horoscopes, tarot readings, crystal and color suggestions, compatibility readings, your natal chart, your Life Path, your "Becoming" vision, and your conversations with Luna (our AI guide) are generated for you and cached so they load quickly and are not regenerated unnecessarily. Your Ask Luna messages and the AI replies are stored so your conversation has history and context.

Friend graph. If you use friends features, we store a friend code for you, the friend requests you send and receive, and your accepted friendships. For each friendship we store a small profile summary (display name, sun sign, friend code) so your friends can see who you are. Accepted friends can see your sun sign, chart basics, and your shared daily content. They cannot see or change your account.

Subscription status. If you purchase Lunara Premium, our payments provider (RevenueCat, working with the Apple App Store) tells us whether your subscription is active. We store a premium flag on your account to unlock features. We never receive or store your full payment card details. Apple processes the payment.

How your data is used
To generate and personalize your astrology and tarot content.
To power Ask Luna conversations with memory of the discussion.
To run friends features (codes, requests, friendships, shared daily content).
To unlock premium features for active subscribers.
To send the daily reminder notification, if you turn it on. Notification preferences and scheduling are handled on your device.
We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third parties we share data with
Lunara relies on a few trusted service providers. Data is shared only as needed for the app to function:

Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging): stores your profile, readings, conversations, and friend graph, and runs our secure server functions. See Google's privacy policy at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
Anthropic (Claude AI): generates your readings and Luna replies. The relevant context (such as your sun sign, birth details, and your Ask Luna messages) is sent to Anthropic's API through our server to produce a response. Our Anthropic API key is held only on our server and never in the app. See Anthropic's privacy policy at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
RevenueCat (subscription management): manages your subscription entitlement and tells our server when it changes. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
Apple (App Store / in-app purchases): processes payments for Lunara Premium. See Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy.
Data retention and deletion
Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. Because accounts are anonymous, we cannot look up your data by name or email. To request deletion of the data associated with your current account, contact lunaradailyapp@gmail.com from the device in question, or use any in-app delete option where available. Removing the app does not by itself delete server-stored data tied to your anonymous ID.

Children
Lunara is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security
Access to your data is controlled by security rules tied to your authenticated account, and sensitive operations run on our server. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on our hosted policy page.

Contact
